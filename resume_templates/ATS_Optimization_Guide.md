# Master ATS Resume Optimization Guide & Checklist

## Why ATS Formatting Matters
Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and Taleo parse resumes into structured plain-text database profiles before any human recruiter reads them. Incorrect formatting can cause your resume to be misparsed or scored as 0% match.

---

## 1. The 7 Golden Rules of ATS Compliance
1. **Single-Column Layout Only**: Never use multi-column tables, text boxes, or split-screen sidebars. ATS parsers read left-to-right across columns, jumbling text together.
2. **Standard Section Headers**:
   - Use standard names: `PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY`, `TECHNICAL SKILLS`, `PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE`, `PROJECTS`, `EDUCATION`, `CERTIFICATIONS`.
   - Avoid creative headings like `My Journey`, `Where I've Been`, or `Toolbox`.
3. **Clean Font Selection**: Use ATS-safe fonts (Calibri, Arial, Helvetica, Georgia, Times New Roman, Roboto) sized between 10pt and 11.5pt.
4. **Standard Bullet Points**: Use standard solid round bullets (`•`). Avoid icons, arrows (`➜`), or checkboxes.
5. **No Graphics, Charts, or Progress Bars**: ATS cannot read "Python: ★★★★★" or progress bars. Write: `Python (Advanced)`.
6. **Chronological Experience Structure**:
   `[Job Title] | [Company Name] — [City, State] | [Month Year – Month Year / Present]`
7. **Export as PDF or .docx**: Ensure text is selectable when highlighting with a cursor. If you cannot highlight and copy text, the ATS cannot read it.

---

## 2. High-Impact Bullet Formula (Google XYZ Method)
Every bullet in your experience section should follow the proven formula:
> **Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]**

### Examples:
- **Weak**: *Worked on LLM chat application with Python.*
- **Strong (ATS-Optimized)**: *Architected enterprise multi-turn LLM assistant using `gemini-3.8-flash` and FastAPI, serving 200,000+ monthly requests with 99.9% uptime.*

- **Weak**: *Helped cut company API costs.*
- **Strong (ATS-Optimized)**: *Implemented sliding-window conversation pruning and token caching, decreasing token overhead by 42% and saving $18,000 annually.*

---

## 3. High-Value Action Verbs for AI & Software Roles
- **Architecture & System Design**: *Architected, Engineered, Designed, Scaled, Overhauled, Deployed*
- **Optimization & Performance**: *Optimized, Accelerated, Streamlined, Reduced, Maximized, Halved*
- **Leadership & Delivery**: *Spearheaded, Directed, Orchestrated, Mentored, Delivered, Standardized*
